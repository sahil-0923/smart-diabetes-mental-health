DAILY NUTRITION INTELLIGENCE

Smart eating principles

Practical education for your dashboard.

🥦

Make vegetables prominent

Include plenty of non-starchy vegetables as part of balanced meals.

💪

Include protein

Protein foods can help create a more balanced meal alongside carbohydrate and fiber-rich foods.

🌾

Watch carbohydrate portions

Carbohydrate quantity and timing matter, particularly for people using insulin.

🍎

Prefer whole fruit

Whole fruit generally provides more fiber than juice. Fruit still contributes carbohydrate.

💧

Hydrate smartly

Water is a simple default. Sugar-sweetened drinks can add substantial carbohydrate.

🕐

Keep your routine consistent

Regular meal patterns can make it easier to understand how meals relate to your glucose records.

⚠️ Important health note

DiaMind AI provides educational nutrition guidance and project-based recommendations. It does not replace an individualized medical nutrition plan.

People with diabetes may have different carbohydrate, calorie and medication requirements. People using insulin should follow the carbohydrate-counting and insulin plan provided by their diabetes-care team.

Do not use this page to independently calculate or change insulin doses. People with kidney disease, pregnancy, allergies or other medical conditions may require additional dietary modifications.
