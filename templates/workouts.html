⚠️ Important safety information

This application does not provide medical clearance for exercise and does not prescribe insulin or medication changes.

If you use insulin or certain glucose-lowering medications, exercise can cause hypoglycemia during or after activity. Follow your personal diabetes-care plan and carry appropriate treatment for lows when advised. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

For very high glucose, particularly in type 1 diabetes, follow your diabetes team's ketone-testing guidance. If ketones are present, vigorous exercise should be avoided. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

People with significant retinopathy, neuropathy, cardiovascular disease, autonomic neuropathy, kidney disease or active foot problems may need an individualized exercise assessment before increasing intensity. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
